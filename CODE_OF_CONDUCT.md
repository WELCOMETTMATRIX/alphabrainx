# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in the Alpha Brain project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

Unacceptable behaviour includes:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Posting financial advice as if it were sanctioned by the project.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and may ban contributors for repeated or severe violations.

Report issues privately to **xapp431@gmail.com**. Reports are handled confidentially and typically acknowledged within 48 hours.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
